# Vanguard — "Frontline Gunner"

**Role in the game:** the squad's all-rounder and usually the first hero a player meets. He holds the wall with an automatic rifle, fires faster when enemies crowd the wall (*Adrenaline*), and his ability, **Overdrive**, makes him and the squad fire faster for a few seconds. He should read instantly as "the dependable lead" even at about 50 pixels tall on a phone.

## Who he is

A young, cocky wasteland defender who volunteered to stand at the wall first. Bright, confident, a little reckless, always the loudest voice on the line. Not a super-soldier: his gear is patched and scuffed. Think scrappy comic hero, not grim military realism.

## Look

- **Proportions:** chibi. Big head, short body, thick limbs, big hands and boots. The silhouette (round helmet, rifle sticking up on the right) must be recognisable as a solid black shape.
- **Helmet:** rounded, orange-red, with a bright yellow visor band across the eyes. Round goggles or glowing amber eyes behind the visor, so his face always has life in it. A small highlight streak on the helmet.
- **Armour:** a chunky red-orange chest plate over a darker red under-suit. Dark knee and elbow guards. A diagonal **bandolier** across the chest, studded with yellow ammo rounds. This is his signature detail.
- **Weapon:** a tall automatic rifle held upright against his shoulder, in dark steel with a lighter metal grip. Long barrel and a visible muzzle, so the muzzle flash has an obvious place to start.
- **Face:** warm light skin. A grin or a small scar is welcome if it survives at small sizes.

## Poses and mood

- **Idle:** feet planted, rifle upright, weight a little forward, a slight cocky tilt of the head. It loops with a small up and down bob.
- **Fire:** a short recoil kick backward with a bright starburst muzzle flash.
- **Overdrive:** the same pose, but wrapped in a warm golden aura with sparks and speed streaks rising around him. He looks energised and grinning.
- **Icon:** head and shoulders, facing the viewer, a confident smirk, the rifle barrel just visible behind his shoulder.

## Skins (later, optional)

*Standard Issue* (red, above), *Arctic Ops* (icy blue and white), *Gilded Guard* (gold and black). Same character, new palettes and a few trim changes.

## Avoid

Gore, dark military grit, thin outlines, and tiny details that vanish at phone size.
